| G-13 | high (v2 only) | **Scope reclassified 2026-07-22:** the ADR-0011 local-model analysis (PT-01 + PT-06) shows G-13 is **not required for the v1 local single-host OFD lease** — a dead holder cannot race a new holder, and a paused holder is a liveness stall (`FM-23`), not split-brain. G-13 now blocks **only** ADR-0011 Decision B (distributed / NFS / multi-host, deferred to v2). It is **not closed** — it remains a valid v2 gap. Two related sub-gaps: (a) **Revocation-proof epoch evidence.** A journal entry from a prior epoch cannot be discarded by a simple `leaseEpoch < current` comparison — the entry may be legitimate committed history. To quarantine an entry, the implementation must prove it was written **after** its epoch was revoked or never reached a valid commit. This requires a protocol extension (e.g., `commitTimestamp`, `revocationNonce`, or a epoch-ordered commit log) that the current SCH-04 schema does not provide. (b) **Conditional atomic commit.** Even with revocation-proof epochs, concern 4 (fence at commit) requires the storage layer to atomically validate the epoch token inside the same I/O that writes the data. Most filesystems (ext4/XFS, NFS) do not expose a conditional atomic commit primitive. | Prototype (extending PT-06) must demonstrate a revocation-proof epoch protocol and whether the journal-scope post-hoc approach (concern 5) is sufficient for Koquetel's workloads or a kernel-level conditional-write primitive is needed. | ADR-0011 Decision B (v2 distributed/NFS/multi-host) only — not v1 |
